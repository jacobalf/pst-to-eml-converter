PstToEmlConverter — Important

This application converts Outlook PST archives into individual .eml files.

REQUIREMENT: Desktop Microsoft Outlook must be installed on this computer.
PstToEmlConverter works by driving Outlook through COM automation; it is not a
standalone PST parser. If desktop Outlook (the classic Win32 app, not the
"new Outlook" / web app) is not installed, the converter will not run.

No internet connection is required for the conversion itself.
